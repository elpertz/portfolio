## Background & Challenge

As part of a new profitability-driven strategy, Addi decided to end its "3 interest-free installments" promise and reinvent its credit offering. The challenge was to do so without damaging brand perception or hindering the planned growth for the year.

## Impact & Results

- Transitioned from a static credit model to a dynamic offering of 1 to 6 installments, with the following distribution post-launch:
  - 56% in 3 installments
  - 11% in 1 or 2 installments
  - 3.5% in 5 installments
  - 29.5% in 6 installments
- Increased approval flexibility, thanks to varying rates based on Addi's risk assessment of each customer.
- 7 percentage point increase in conversion, attributed to the clarity of the new flow and payment options.
- Significant rise in customer NPS: from 76 to 85.
- More sustainable business model, with better balance between risk and revenue.
