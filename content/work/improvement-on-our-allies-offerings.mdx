## 🎯 El Desafío

There was a wide variety of offers and rates within Addi, causing confusion among merchants regarding their payment options and commissions. There was also concern about potential massive churn due to the need to migrate them.

> **Contexto importante:** This project required careful balance between business needs and merchant satisfaction.

## 📊 Resultados Medibles

### Impacto Financiero

- **USD 1.2 million** in additional annual revenue.
- Less than **2% of allies** discontinued the service, surpassing churn expectations.

### Satisfacción del Usuario

- NPS continued to rise after migration to the new plans.
- A group of "cold allies" with nearly no sales significantly reactivated their sales thanks to the flexibility of the new plans.

## 💡 Lecciones Aprendidas

Durante este proyecto descubrí que:

- La comunicación temprana y constante con stakeholders es crucial
- Los merchants valoran más la **claridad** que las opciones complejas
- Las métricas de satisfacción son tan importantes como las financieras
