# Improvement on Our Allies' Offerings

<CaseImage src="/images/brand-exm.png" alt="Main project image" />

## Problem Summary

There was a wide variety of offers and rates within Addi, causing confusion among merchants regarding their payment options and commissions. There was also concern about potential massive churn due to the need to migrate them.

## My Role

- Facilitated alignment sessions with Finance, Revenue, and Product teams.
- Led research with merchants and tested new payment plans.
- Designed the user experience in the portal to centralize offers and commissions.
- Developed the communication and plan offering strategy.

## Key Metrics

- USD 1.2 million in additional annual revenue.
- Less than 2% of allies discontinued the service, surpassing churn expectations.
- NPS continued to rise after migration to the new plans.
- A group of "cold allies" with nearly no sales (X%) significantly reactivated their sales thanks to the flexibility of the new plans.
